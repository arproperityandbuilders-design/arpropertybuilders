Frequently asked questions

Answers about our construction process, property purchases and investment terms.

How long does a typical residential project take?

Most mid-rise residential projects take 24–30 months from groundbreaking to handover, depending on scale, site conditions and approval timelines.

Do you offer construction-only contracts without design?

Yes. Clients with existing architectural drawings can engage us purely for construction and project management services.

What is the minimum investment for a property partnership?

Investment thresholds vary by project. Our investment consultancy team can walk you through current opportunities and expected returns.

Are your buildings covered by a structural warranty?

Every project includes a structural warranty period, with details specified in your handover documentation.

Can I customise the interior finishing of my unit?

Yes, our interior design team offers finishing packages and bespoke customisation options prior to handover.

How do I schedule a site visit?

Contact our team via phone, email or the contact form and we'll arrange a convenient time for a guided site visit.

What legal documentation is provided at handover?

We provide title deeds, occupancy certificates and all regulatory approvals required for full legal ownership transfer.

Do you provide after-sales facility management?

Yes, our facility management division offers ongoing maintenance, security and tenant services after handover.

Still have questions?

Our team is ready to answer anything specific to your project or investment plans.
